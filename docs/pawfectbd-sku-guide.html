Pawfect-BD SKU Guide

One short, readable SKU for every product and variant. Built to print as a barcode on the 50×32 mm label.

1. The format

5LVariant (optional)

Example: CLT-0301-5L = Cat · LT litter · brand 03 (Captain Mew) · product 01 · 5L pack.

Product SKU = 8 characters. With a variant = at most 13 characters (variant is 1–4 characters). That is the longest that prints cleanly on the label.

Pet letter

CCat
DDog
BBird
AAll pets / other (e.g. universal accessories)

The 4-digit number = brand + product

First 2 digits are the brand slot (same brand = same slot in every category). Last 2 digits are the product number within that brand and category (01–99).

Cozie is slot 01, so every Cozie litter is CLT-01xx and every Cozie cat food is CFD-01xx.

2. The golden rule: product or variant?

New product (new number)

Anything that changes what it is: flavour, scent, type, formula.

Cozie Lavender and Cozie Lemon are two products: CLT-0101 and CLT-0102.

Variant (suffix)

Anything that changes how much or what size: weight, volume, pack count, clothing size, colour.

The 5 L and 10 L of Cozie Lavender: CLT-0101-5L, CLT-0101-10L.

Many brands share the same sizes. That is fine: CLT-0101-5L (Cozie), CLT-0201-5L (Clean Step) and CLT-0301-5L (Captain Mew) are all different SKUs because the brand slot comes before the size. The size suffix is the same for everyone, so it stays standard.

3. Brand slots

One slot per brand, shared across all pets and categories. Slot 00 is for your own / unbranded products. Edit the names below, they are saved in this browser and used by the builder. Never reuse or reassign a slot.

8. Rules

  • Uppercase letters, digits and dashes only. No spaces, no Bangla characters.
  • Maximum 13 characters. The barcode label cannot print longer SKUs at a width a scanner can read.
  • One SKU = one sellable thing. Never reuse a SKU, even for a discontinued product. Keep counting up.
  • Add a new brand by taking the next free slot, then write it in the brand list above.
  • A brand with more than 99 products in one category: give it a second slot in the register (e.g. “Cozie 2”).
  • Brand and flavour live in the product name too, so staff can find a product by searching either the SKU or the name.
